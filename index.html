Elevating Barbering from a Routine to an Art Form.

At Nerouppos Barber Shop, we believe a haircut is the foundation of a man’s presence. Located in the heart of Kato Polemidia, our atelier combines timeless Mediterranean grooming hospitality with surgical scissor technique.

Whether you demand a zero-skin fade with flawless blending, a hot lather straight-razor shave, or deliberate beard architecture, you step into an environment cultivated for calm, precision, and confidence.
